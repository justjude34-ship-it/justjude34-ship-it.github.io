How the Chart Works

The Pythagorean birth chart is a 3 × 3 grid of the digits 1 to 9. Your date of birth fills it, and the shape of what is filled — and what is left empty — is the reading.

Write the date as DD/MM/YYYY. Example: 29/11/1968 becomes 2, 9, 1, 1, 1, 9, 6, 8. Zeros occupy no square. Digits stack. An empty square is a lesson, not a sentence.
